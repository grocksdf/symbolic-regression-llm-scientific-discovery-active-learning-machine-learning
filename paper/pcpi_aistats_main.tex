\begin{abstract}
Scientific laws are rarely identifiable from a single observational fit:
distinct symbolic structures can be predictively equivalent on the available
domain, while equally accurate interpolants can disagree under a targeted
measurement. We formulate symbolic scientific discovery as sequential
Bayesian discrimination among operational predictive-equivalence classes.
The inferential target is a joint posterior over symbolic structure,
continuous parameters, and observation-noise parameters. \method{} is designed
to approximate this posterior with target-correct sequential Monte Carlo and
to select measurements by their expected information about predictive
classes. An exactly enumerable finite bank supplies a reference posterior on
provenance-verified measured observations. A fixed-universe resample--move SMC
implementation is compared with that reference before any open-grammar claim.
The original 72-run CCPP and grouped Gas Turbine CO/NOX finite-bank check passed
its preregistered exact-posterior thresholds but exposed severe long-horizon
particle genealogy coalescence. A Rao--Blackwellized, adaptively tempered
implementation repairs that failure mode, and an exact-reference diagnostic
validates an explicit proposal-corrected collapsed trans-dimensional kernel.
We validate a Student-t-measure Gauss--Jacobi class-EIG estimator against
independent quadrature under the repaired operational-class definition. The
latest 96-run measured-pool experiment passed every protocol and evidence
check but did not establish predictive or structural advantage. On CCPP,
frozen-class gain relative to random acquisition was $-0.2838$ (95\% interval
$[-0.5047,-0.0629]$), with negative transfer in 7/8 seeds; the grouped Gas
Turbine interval crossed zero. A discrepancy profile activated on Gas but did
not repair the family-level result. We retain these outcomes as negative
development evidence and use them to motivate a reference-dominance decision
rule that acts target-seekingly only when its improvement over a registered
reference policy is numerically certified. Its present evidence is restricted
to exact-reference correctness; real superiority, motif transfer, and
held-out effectiveness claims remain blocked.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Symbolic regression is often evaluated as one-shot expression fitting. That
view hides two statistical difficulties. First, algebraic syntax is not the
scientific estimand: several structures may induce the same predictions over
the domain where decisions are made. Second, new measurements are chosen
adaptively, so uncertainty must be updated along the realized action--outcome
history rather than recomputed from an opaque fitness population.

We instead treat discovery as sequential Bayesian discrimination. At each
round the learner maintains uncertainty over symbolic structure, coefficients,
and nuisance parameters; maps structure mass to predictive classes; selects an
action intended to separate posterior-supported classes; and incorporates only
the newly observed likelihood factor. The terminal output is a Bayes decision
over predictive classes. Posterior predictive risk is reported separately as
external validity.

The paper is organized around three primary contributions.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=1pt,topsep=2pt]
  \item A sequential Bayesian formulation whose primary estimand is an
  operational predictive-equivalence class rather than an expression string.
  \item A target-correct SMC construction over symbolic structures,
  coefficients, and noise parameters, validated against an exactly enumerable
  posterior before any open-grammar claim is made. \evidencegate{P2}
  \item A target-aware acquisition rule whose class-EIG semantics, numerical
  ranking, and improvement over a registered reference policy are separately
  certified; an uncertified decision returns to that policy instead of
  switching to a different scientific objective. \evidencegate{P3}
\end{itemize}

Reliability-gated motif proposals are optional. They enter the paper only if
their complete proposal correction preserves the posterior target. LLMs and
multi-engine orchestration remain proposal and systems mechanisms, not parts
of the statistical target.

\section{Related Work}

\paragraph{Bayesian symbolic inference.}
Bayesian symbolic regression and probabilistic program induction place priors
over structured programs and integrate or sample their parameters. Our focus
is the adaptive sequence of posterior targets and the pushforward from
structures to predictive classes, while distinguishing a normalized posterior
from heuristic fitness normalization.

\paragraph{Sequential Monte Carlo.}
SMC combines incremental importance weights, unbiased resampling, and
target-invariant move kernels for a sequence of distributions
\cite{doucet2001smc,delmoral2006smc}. We use these results only after the
symbolic implementation verifies support, correction, and genealogy.

\paragraph{Bayesian experimental design.}
Expected information gain provides a decision-theoretic reference for
measurement selection \cite{chaloner1995bayesian,foster2021bax}. Predictive
uncertainty is not generally equivalent to structural information: high
aleatoric noise can increase the former without separating symbolic laws.
Robust BED instead optimizes information under an explicit ambiguity set
\cite{go2022robust,abdulsamad2026maximin}; this is distinct from treating a
small collection of likelihood powers as a universal misspecification model.

\paragraph{Reference-relative decisions.}
Conservative exploration and safe policy improvement return to a registered
baseline when an alternative is not sufficiently supported
\cite{katariya2019interleaving,laroche2019spibb}. Our use is narrower: the
reference is an acquisition policy over the same visible pool, and the
handover certificate concerns model-based information about the frozen
scientific estimand. It is not a physical-safety or misspecification-robustness
guarantee.

\paragraph{Reusable symbolic structure.}
Library learning and compositional program induction motivate reusable motifs
\cite{ellis2021dreamcoder,lake2015humanlevel}. In \method{}, reuse is restricted
to a corrected proposal mixture with source-only reliability; it cannot alter
the likelihood or posterior target.

\section{Problem Formulation}

\subsection{Adaptive history and posterior}

Let $S\in\mathcal S$ be a canonical symbolic structure,
$\theta_S$ its continuous parameters, and $\psi$ observation-noise or nuisance
parameters. The history after $t$ acquired measurements is
\[
H_t=H_0\cup\{(a_i,x_i,y_i)\}_{i=1}^t.
\]
Untouched-heldout observations are not part of $H_t$ and are unavailable to
the algorithm. The joint posterior is
\[
\pi_t(S,\theta_S,\psi)
=p(S,\theta_S,\psi\mid H_t)
\propto p(S)p(\theta_S\mid S,\psi)p(\psi)
\prod_{o\in H_t}p(y_o\mid a_o,x_o,S,\theta_S,\psi).
\]
The structure marginal is
\[
p_t(S)=\int\pi_t(S,\theta_S,\psi)\,d\theta_S\,d\psi.
\]

The first target-correct implementation uses
\[
y=f_S(a,x;\theta_S)+\varepsilon,
\qquad \varepsilon\sim\mathcal N(0,\sigma^2),
\]
with $\theta_S\mid S,\sigma^2$ Gaussian and $\sigma^2$ Inverse-Gamma.
Heteroscedastic and generalized-Bayes variants are outside the current target.

\subsection{Operational predictive classes}

At round $t$, let $\mathcal A_t^{\mathrm{op}}$ be a frozen, selection-visible
action domain. We compare posterior-predictive 0.1/0.5/0.9 quantiles over this
domain using their root-mean-square difference, standardized pointwise by the
pooled posterior-predictive standard deviation. Deterministic complete-link
clustering with preregistered diameter $\epsilon_B$ yields disjoint operational
classes without epsilon chaining. For future measurement budget $B$, the real
protocol sets $\epsilon_B=1/\sqrt{B}$, so equivalent laws have root-budget
aggregate standardized separation at most one. This resolution is independent
of dataset and target identity. The
initial posterior and visible action domain define a frozen map $C_0(S)$. If
$P_t^{(0)}$ is its posterior pushforward, then
\[
P_t^{(0)}(c)=\sum_{S:C_0(S)=c}p_t(S).
\]
Exact predictive equivalence and alternative thresholds are sensitivity
analyses rather than result-conditioned replacements for the primary
definition. Both acquisition and the comparative endpoint target this same
random variable. The endpoint is
$H(C_0\mid H_0)-H(C_0\mid H_B)$. Recomputed dynamic partitions are
diagnostics only.

For a Student-$t_\nu$ predictive variable $T$,
$\nu/(\nu+T^2)\sim\mathrm{Beta}(\nu/2,1/2)$. We therefore integrate the
antithetic sign average directly with a Gauss--Jacobi rule. The real-pool
policy doubles its evaluation budget from 32 to at most 512. The point-estimate
leader is certified only when its lower nested-quadrature error envelope
exceeds every competitor's upper envelope. This is an exact-reference-validated
asymptotic numerical diagnostic, not a rigorous quadrature bound or a
finite-sample confidence sequence. If a unique EIG maximizer is not certified,
the repaired rule uses posterior epistemic variance of the latent mean,
excluding observation noise, and records that the utility is not EIG.

\subsection{Terminal decision}

For preregistered structural loss $L$, the terminal decision after budget $B$
is
\[
\widehat c_B\in\arg\min_{\widehat c}
\E_{c\sim P_B}[L(c,\widehat c)].
\]
The default is $0$--$1$ class loss. Predictive risk is a secondary estimand and
cannot retroactively alter this decision rule.

\section{Posterior Computation}

\subsection{Exact finite-universe reference}

Before SMC, define a small finite bank
$\mathcal S_{\mathrm{ref}}=\{S_1,\ldots,S_K\}$ in which every structure is
linear in its coefficients,
$f_S(x;\theta_S)=\phi_S(x)^\top\theta_S$. With
\[
\theta_S\mid\sigma^2\sim
\mathcal N(m_0,\sigma^2\Lambda_0^{-1}),\qquad
\sigma^2\sim\mathrm{IG}(\alpha_0,\beta_0),
\]
the marginal likelihood is available analytically. Let
\[
\begin{aligned}
\Lambda_n&=\Lambda_0+\Phi^\top\Phi,\\
m_n&=\Lambda_n^{-1}(\Lambda_0m_0+\Phi^\top y),\\
\alpha_n&=\alpha_0+n/2,\\
\beta_n&=\beta_0+\tfrac12(y^\top y+m_0^\top\Lambda_0m_0
-m_n^\top\Lambda_nm_n).
\end{aligned}
\]
Then
\[
\log p(y\mid S)=
-\frac n2\log(2\pi)+\frac12(\log|\Lambda_0|-\log|\Lambda_n|)
+\alpha_0\log\beta_0-\alpha_n\log\beta_n
+\log\Gamma(\alpha_n)-\log\Gamma(\alpha_0).
\]
The implementation cross-checks this expression by integrating $\sigma^2$
numerically after independently integrating the coefficients.

The bank is generated only from input dimension and is identical for tasks
with the same number of covariates. Dataset and target names do not select its
structures. Exact-reference comparison on registered measured observations is
kept separate from small controlled correctness fixtures. The latter may
support only normalization, integration, SMC, and EIG correctness and are
never real-data efficacy evidence. Historical generated-observation efficacy
runs are excluded. Even a passed finite-bank check cannot by itself support
scientific-discovery efficacy or comparison with external methods.

\subsection{SMC target sequence}

For $z=(S,\theta_S,\psi)$ and newly acquired likelihood factor
$g_t(z)=p(y_t\mid a_t,x_t,z)$, define
\[
\pi_t(z)\propto p_0(z)\prod_{r=1}^{t}g_r(z).
\]
Particles initialized from $q_0$ receive corrected initial weights. Later
weights multiply only by $g_t$. ESS-triggered unbiased resampling records
ancestor indices, after which a rejuvenation kernel leaves $\pi_t$ invariant.
Birth, death, and structural replacement moves require explicit forward and
reverse probabilities, dimension matching, and any Jacobian term.
\evidencegate{P2A exact-posterior convergence and P2B trans-dimensional correction}

For the robust finite-bank implementation, $(\theta_S,\sigma^2)$ are
Rao--Blackwellized and
$g_t(S)=p(y_t\mid a_t,x_t,S,H_{t-1})$ is computed from independent conjugate
sufficient statistics. An informative observation is bridged through
\[
\eta_{t,\beta}(S)\propto p_{t-1}(S)g_t(S)^\beta,
\qquad 0=\beta_0<\cdots<\beta_B=1,
\]
with temperatures selected by a frozen conditional-ESS target. Conditional
coefficient and noise draws at $\beta=1$ reconstruct joint posterior
particles. This changes the numerical path but not the terminal target.

For P2B, the collapsed structure proposal contains explicit birth, death, and
equal-dimensional replacement edges. If $q(S'\mid S)$ denotes the catalog
probability, the rejuvenation acceptance probability is
\[
\alpha(S,S')=\min\!\left\{1,
\frac{\eta_{t,\beta}(S')q(S\mid S')}
     {\eta_{t,\beta}(S)q(S'\mid S)}|J|\right\}.
\]
Coefficients and noise are integrated out during the move, so dimension
matching is the empty auxiliary map and $|J|=1$. This is a corrected finite
collapsed trans-dimensional kernel; it is not yet a claim about arbitrary
open-grammar subtree RJ moves. \evidencegate{P2B frozen exact-reference Gate}

\section{Predictive-Class Acquisition}

The acquisition objective is class information gain,
\[
\mathrm{EIG}^{(0)}_t(a)=H(C_0\mid H_t)-
\E_{Y\sim p_t(Y\mid a)}[H(C_0\mid H_t,a,Y)].
\]
In the finite conjugate bank, each structure-conditional predictive density is
Student-$t$. Hence
\[
I(C_0;Y_a\mid H_t)=
\sum_c P_t^{(0)}(c)\int p_t(y\mid c,a)
\log\frac{p_t(y\mid c,a)}{p_t(y\mid a)}\,dy,
\]
where $p_t(y\mid c,a)$ is the posterior-weighted mixture over structures in
class $c$. We compute the reference by adaptive integration on the real line.
The production estimator pushes each Student-$t$ predictive measure to its
Beta square transform and applies an antithetic Gauss--Jacobi rule, allocating
half of the node budget uniformly over structures and the remainder by
posterior mass. Exact mixture densities are used in the log ratio. The P3A.2
Gate compares normalized error, error-envelope coverage, rank correlation,
top-one agreement, false certification, and convergence against the independent
reference over increasing quadrature budgets.

P3B.7 activates this class score at every PCPI query, yet its returned real
run does not pass the efficacy Gate. This is consistent with the distinction
between information about a model index and information about predictions on
the target input distribution \cite{bickfordsmith2023prediction}, and with
recent BOED analyses of selection-induced covariate shift under
misspecification \cite{forster2025generalization}. P3B.8 therefore lets
$X^\star$ be uniform on the registered selection-visible action domain and
targets the chain rule
\[
I(C_0,Y^\star;Y_a\mid H_t)
=I(C_0;Y_a\mid H_t)+I(Y^\star;Y_a\mid C_0,H_t).
\]
The first term remains the validated class-EIG. For the second, we match the
first two moments of each class-conditional finite posterior mixture and
average the resulting bivariate-Gaussian mutual information over $X^\star$.
This is exact for a Gaussian class-conditional predictive and is explicitly
named a Gaussian-moment surrogate for the production Student-$t$ mixture. The
terms share information units, so no result-fitted tradeoff weight is added.
If the adaptive envelope cannot certify the joint-score leader, the rule
maximizes $\Var_{S,\theta_S\mid H_t}[f_S(a;\theta_S)]$, excluding observation
noise and reporting the branch as posterior-epistemic acquisition.
The returned P3B.8 run uses a certified joint score throughout but fails the
efficacy Gate. P3B.9 therefore constrains this unchanged score to candidates
whose addition does not increase biased RBF-kernel MMD between the observed
design and the fixed registered action domain. Coordinates and the
median-distance bandwidth use covariates only. If the safe set is empty, the
minimum-MMD candidate is selected and recorded as an explicit fallback.
The returned P3B.9 run applies this guard throughout but still fails efficacy.
P3B.10 therefore ranks each safe candidate by the minimum P3B.9 joint utility
over the likelihood-power family $\{0.125,0.25,0.5,1\}$ frozen before this
repair. The calibrated nominal posterior remains the reporting and evaluation
posterior; the finite family changes PCPI ranking only. A robust leader is
certified by lower-envelope interval dominance, and least-favorable-model ties
are resolved toward the smaller likelihood power.
\evidencegate{P3B.10 maximin correctness diagnostic and heldout-closed real rerun}

Because that real rerun remains negative, P3C.1 treats the next repair as a
predictive model-discrepancy problem. It reconstructs a posterior-weighted
residual mean square from acquired-history sufficient statistics, subtracts
the prior mean noise variance with a zero floor, and distributes the resulting
extra variance according to covariate distance from the acquired design. The
registered target domain fixes both coordinate scaling and the median-distance
bandwidth. Candidate, validation, and held-out responses do not enter this
profile. Student-$t$ component scales are moment-matched to the inflated
variance before applying the unchanged finite maximin joint utility and MMD
safe set. Controlled tests support implementation correctness only; the
heldout-closed P3C.1 run completes 96/96 policies but fails the efficacy Gate.
The discrepancy is effectively zero on CCPP and active but insufficient on
Gas; it is retained as a failed repair rather than promoted as robustness.
\evidencegate{P3C.1 protocol passed; real efficacy failed}

P3D.1 returns to the primary class-EIG and registers a reference action policy
$q_t$ over the same visible pool. Given containing utility intervals
$[L_t(a),R_t(a)]$, it selects the action with largest lower bound only when
$L_t(\widehat a) > \sum_a q_t(a)R_t(a)+\tau_{\rm num}$; otherwise it samples
from $q_t$. Thus an unresolved ranking abstains to the registered policy rather
than switching to epistemic variance. P3D.1 is implemented only in an exactly
enumerable finite-outcome diagnostic and is not imported by the real runtime.
Its clean-source controlled Gate passes all 14 frozen correctness decisions.
\evidencegate{P3D.1 correctness fixture passed; real integration absent}

\section{Theory and Scope}

\begin{assumption}[Predictable acquisition]
$a_t$ is measurable with respect to $H_{t-1}$ and does not depend on an
unobserved future outcome.
\end{assumption}

Conditioned on the realized predictable actions, the likelihood factorization
above remains valid. The theory section will separate this adaptive-posterior
validity statement from SMC approximation error.

P3B.5 introduced the explicitly named
generalized posterior
\[
\pi_{t,\eta}(z)\propto p(z)\prod_{i=1}^t p(y_i\mid x_i,z)^\eta,
\qquad 0<\eta\le1.
\]
One $\eta$ is chosen by prequential posterior-expected posterior-randomized
log loss (R-log SafeBayes; \cite{grunwald2017inconsistency}) on the initial development observations and then shared by every
acquisition policy. Every closed basis term is centered and scaled from the
initial-development covariates before the shared coefficient prior is applied.
This target is not described as ordinary Bayes when $\eta<1$.
The returned P3B.5 run cannot be used as efficacy evidence: fitting used the
frozen preconditioned design while posterior prediction and acquisition rebuilt
raw basis rows. P3B.6 makes the posterior engine's frozen design transform the
single coordinate map for fitting, posterior predictive distributions,
validation metrics, operational classes, and all acquisition policies.
Its returned real run passes the protocol Gate but not the efficacy Gate: the
one-SD operational radius collapses most initial class mass. P3B.7 derives the
radius from the registered future measurement budget and activates class-EIG
throughout, but its returned 96/96 real run still fails the family-level
efficacy Gate. P3B.8 changes the acquisition estimand, not the posterior,
structure bank, class radius, budgets, seeds, splits, or assessment rule. Its
returned real run also fails efficacy despite certified joint rankings. P3B.9
adds a covariate-only representative safe set but its returned run still fails
efficacy. P3B.10 retains the nominal posterior and every protocol commitment,
changing only PCPI ranking to the finite lower envelope described above.

\begin{proposition}[Reference-policy dominance]
Let $U_t(a)=I(C_0;Y_a\mid H_t)$ and suppose
$L_t(a)\le U_t(a)\le R_t(a)$ simultaneously for all visible actions. Define
$U_t(q)=\sum_a q_t(a)U_t(a)$ and analogously $R_t(q)$. If P3D.1 hands over to
$\widehat a$ because
$L_t(\widehat a)>R_t(q)+\tau_{\rm num}$, then
$U_t(\widehat a)>U_t(q)$. If it does not hand over, its expected utility equals
$U_t(q)$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
In the handover branch,
$U_t(\widehat a)\ge L_t(\widehat a)>R_t(q)\ge U_t(q)$. In the reference branch,
linearity of expectation under $A\sim q_t$ gives
$\E[U_t(A)]=\sum_a q_t(a)U_t(a)=U_t(q)$.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Zero-capacity abstention]
If $H(C_0\mid H_t)=0$, every class-EIG is zero and no valid interval system can
certify strict reference dominance. P3D.1 therefore executes $q_t$ without a
fitted entropy threshold.
\end{proposition}

Both statements are conditional on the frozen posterior and valid simultaneous
intervals. The current Gauss--Jacobi fine/coarse discrepancy is an asymptotic
diagnostic, not a rigorous bound; consequently these propositions do not yet
authorize a real-run no-harm claim.

Under shared Gaussian observation noise, a maximum-entropy argument gives
\[
I(Z;Y_a\mid H_t)\le
\frac12\log\left(1+\frac{\Var(\mu_Z(a)\mid H_t)}{\sigma^2(a)}\right).
\]
The final theorem must distinguish information about the full latent state
$Z$ from information about its predictive-class coarsening. A low-signal
expansion will be included only if its regularity and scaling conditions are
proved. \evidencegate{theorem proofs and counterexample}

Standard SMC consistency is invoked only after the implementation verifies
incremental potentials, support, unbiased resampling, and invariant moves.
No generic Monte Carlo rate is presented as a novel theorem.

\section{Experiments}

\paragraph{RQ1: matched-budget closed-loop effectiveness.}
Compare one-shot fitting, sequential random acquisition, predictive
uncertainty, query by committee, and PCPI under identical observation,
candidate-evaluation, particle, and compute budgets. Report normalized AULC,
final risk, posterior entropy, class concentration, and failure runs.

\paragraph{RQ2: posterior correctness.}
Compare exact posterior and SMC over increasing particle counts. Report class
TV/KL, structure posterior error, predictive NLL, credible coverage, ESS,
ancestor diversity, bridge CESS, immediate-parent concentration, weight
entropy, seed stability, and time. The original P2A run used official CCPP and
Gas Turbine CO/NOX measurements; CO and NOX count as one data family. Its
finite-bank agreement check passed with a genealogy warning. P2B separately
uses a labelled, exactly enumerable diagnostic fixture to test proposal-ratio
correction, detailed balance, invariance, and particle convergence. That
fixture is correctness evidence only and is excluded from real-data efficacy
claims.

\paragraph{RQ3: acquisition effectiveness.}
All policies share one posterior engine. Compare realized entropy reduction,
class-mass gain, score--gain correlation, nAULC, predictive risk, and compute;
use exact class EIG where enumeration is possible.

The P3B protocol uses 32 initial observations, 32 acquired measurements, a
frozen 128-action pool, 256 validation observations, and eight paired seeds.
All four policies share one conjugate finite-bank target to isolate the
acquisition effect. Results are reported with paired 95\% seed confidence
intervals; Gas Turbine CO and NOX count as one dataset family.
P3B.2 completed all 96 policies without leakage and passed every protocol
check, but it did not demonstrate a consistent advantage. All family-level
predictive intervals crossed zero, and frozen-class gain was nonpositive
against every baseline. CCPP class entropy was already numerically zero. That
run is retained as development negative evidence. It also revealed that the
query utility targeted a recomputed $C_t$ while the endpoint targeted $C_0$.
P3B.3 fixed $C_0$ throughout the sequence and passed its protocol and decision
validity checks, but still failed the family-level efficacy Gate. CCPP used the
fallback for 98.8\% of PCPI queries; all grouped Gas efficacy intervals crossed
zero. P3B.4 replaced the adjacent-column bank and added generalized-likelihood
calibration, but its returned 96/96 run also failed efficacy: all CCPP seeds
selected $\eta=1$, CCPP began with collapsed class entropy, and no primary
family-level interval excluded zero. P3B.5 replaced mixture-log-score
calibration with R-log SafeBayes and preconditioned all bank terms using only
initial-development covariates, but its returned efficacy run is invalid due
to the predictive-coordinate mismatch above. P3B.6 repairs that mismatch, then
returns 96/96 protocol-valid runs without strong efficacy evidence: CCPP initial
frozen-class entropy is $7.33\times10^{-12}$ and class-EIG is used in only
0.78\% of PCPI queries. P3B.7 replaces the fixed one-SD radius by the
budget-resolved definition above and uses class-EIG for 100\% of PCPI queries,
but it still fails efficacy. Against random, mean predictive nAULC differences
are $-0.0173$ for CCPP and $-0.0510$ for grouped Gas, with both paired 95\%
intervals crossing zero. Only grouped Gas versus QBC excludes zero
($-0.0262$, 95\% CI $[-0.0501,-0.0023]$); structural-gain intervals are not
consistently positive across families and baselines. P3B.8 addresses the
target-mismatch diagnosis with the joint class--predictive score, but its
returned CCPP frozen-class gain versus random is $-0.3050$ with 95\% CI
$[-0.4652,-0.1447]$. Its selected joint score has pooled Spearman correlation
$0.0571$ with realized local class gain on CCPP. P3B.9 adds the representative
safe set, but its returned CCPP class-gain difference versus random is
$-0.2510$ with 95\% CI $[-0.4617,-0.0403]$, and its pooled selected-score versus
realized-gain Spearman correlation is $-0.0079$. P3B.10 evaluates the frozen
finite lower envelope inside that same guard. Budgets, seeds, nominal models,
and efficacy Gates remain unchanged. The returned P3B.10 run is protocol-valid
with 96/96 completed runs and closed held-out, but CCPP frozen-class gain versus
random is $-0.2838$ (95\% CI $[-0.5047,-0.0629]$), with negative transfer in
7/8 seeds; grouped Gas Turbine gain is not significant. Thus no real
superiority result is claimed, and the next repair must address general
posterior or decision-model misspecification rather than tune the data.
P3C.1 adds a scalar distance-shaped discrepancy variance while preserving the
same splits, budgets, policies, ambiguity set, safe set, and closed held-out.
Its 96/96 run is also protocol-valid and returns the same CCPP paired effect,
because the registered residual-excess variance is at most $6.88\times10^{-13}$
there. The discrepancy is nonzero on Gas CO and NOX (mean selected variances
$0.3281$ and $0.5801$), but grouped Gas frozen-class gain versus random is
$0.0659$ with 95\% interval $[-0.2291,0.3609]$. The scalar discrepancy
envelope is therefore an insufficient repair, not a no-op or a protocol
failure.

\section{Current Evidence Boundary}

operational-class aggregation, Rao--Blackwellized SMC weights, adaptive CESS
bridges, systematic resampling, genealogy, and an explicit proposal-corrected
collapsed trans-dimensional kernel. The P2B fixture supports only RQ2
finite-bank correctness and cannot support real-data efficacy. P3A.2 is the
matching exact-quadrature check for the production EIG estimator and is
likewise a correctness diagnostic. P3B.5 is invalid efficacy evidence; P3B.6
supports coordinate correctness and a valid but insufficient real-development
result. P3B.7 supports budget-resolved class correctness and a valid negative
real result. P3B.8 supports joint-score correctness and a valid negative real
result. P3B.9 supports representative-guard correctness and a valid negative
real result. P3B.10 controlled evidence supports correctness of the finite
maximin decision, while its real rerun supports only protocol-valid negative
development evidence. P3C.1 passed its controlled implementation Gate and
completed a protocol-valid real run, but
\texttt{formal\_efficacy\_evidence=false} and the registered status is
\texttt{REAL\_ADVANTAGE\_NOT\_DEMONSTRATED}. The held-out was

\section{Conclusion}

\method{} reframes symbolic discovery as sequential Bayesian discrimination
among predictive classes. The exact-reference engine makes posterior semantics
and acquisition naming testable before the project invests in open-grammar
SMC. After the corrected P2B, exact P3A.2, P3B.6 coordinate, and P3B.7 class
Gates, the negative P3B.10 and P3C.1 real audits show that numerical validity
does not imply acquisition efficacy. The subsequent controlled Gate validates
the decision contract: select a target-seeking action only when its
interval lower bound dominates the registered reference policy's interval
upper bound, and otherwise execute that reference policy. This establishes a
model-relative correctness property, not a real-world no-harm guarantee. A
production interval construction, full regression on the complete canonical
source, another measured-pool run, motif transfer, and held-out confirmation
remain downstream and independently blocked.
