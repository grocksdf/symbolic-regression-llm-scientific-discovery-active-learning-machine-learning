# Typed Scientist paired-case analysis

The Gas-NOX and Yacht screens are retained as a paired development analysis,
not as a universal superiority claim.

Gas-NOX is the positive structural case: the full bank remained viable, the
typed LLM source was admitted with positive gains on both arbitration folds,
and no-LLM collapsed after the same safety admission.

Yacht is the independent boundary case: the full bank collapsed at the
operational-resolution Gate, the LLM synthesis candidates were not admitted,
and no-LLM achieved lower validation error. This shows that the Scientist
contribution is task-dependent and that the safety layer correctly rejects
negative transfer.

The paired claim is therefore architectural and conditional: the system can
produce a fold-safe structural contribution when evidence supports it, and it
can refuse to promote the same mechanism when evidence does not support it.
Neither case establishes universal predictive superiority, acquisition
efficacy or held-out superiority.
