# Typed Scientist structural-contribution case claim

The Gas-NOX development coordinate is a case study of a specific mechanism:
lineage-bound typed LLM synthesis can preserve a viable operational hypothesis
bank after safety-filtered multi-engine search without the LLM collapses.

The immutable certificate requires:

- positive fold-safe LLM log-score gains on both arbitration folds;
- nonzero admitted LLM source weight with at least half of prior mass reserved
  for the task-independent core;
- viable full and single-engine banks;
- a no-LLM bank that collapses only after the same source-safety admission;
- safe exclusion certificates for every absent optional engine;
- closed candidate responses, reporting validation and held-out data.

This supports an AISTATS case-study claim about system architecture and
hypothesis-bank construction. It does not establish acquisition efficacy,
scientific-law truth, cross-family generality, or held-out superiority.

No threshold, source weight, entropy rule, seed, data role or candidate is
changed in response to the observed result. A later independent confirmation
must use a separately frozen coordinate and a metric that remains defined when
an ablation produces a degenerate bank.
