# Candidate semantic and numerical validity upgrade

This is a correctness/source-composition freeze. It authorizes no real,
held-out, reporting-response or provider-budget experiment.

## Scope

The upgrade acts after candidate generation and before expensive parameter
fitting. The registered real-input grammar still permits division, log, sqrt,
fractional/negative powers, exp and their compositions. Each compiled typed
proposal carries operator obligations; the generation context supplies real
variable types, observed/registered ranges and zero occurrence. Ranges describe
supplied covariate points, not a Cartesian-product guarantee.

Preflight evaluates the original, unsimplified expression and every frozen
basis term on development/selection covariates plus registered action
covariates. It reads no responses, independent-admission covariates, reporting
rows, test/OOD or held-out data. It does not fabricate Cartesian probes that
would violate a joint input constraint. The expanded metric stability proxy
likewise uses registered points. Finite-point definedness is not a proof of
validity throughout a continuous region or at future observations.

Invalid div/log/sqrt/real floating power is `domain_undefined`. Intermediate
or final overflow/underflow, including scalar subexpressions and floating
literal underflow, is `numerical_nonfinite`. Remaining fit exceptions are
`parameter_fit_failed`. No implicit abs, clamp, epsilon, retry, support deletion
or coefficient repair is introduced. Abs remains an explicit grammar operator.

## Shared correctness repairs

* External amplitudes in rational numerators are removed from structure
  identity. Commutative syntax and sqrt/power syntax are normalized without
  cancelling domain factors. Historical v1 tokens remain decodable with their
  original expression and hash; no historical evidence is migrated.
* Regional admission and independent-gap log scoring use the posterior
  engine's native log weights, rather than log of probability values clamped
  above floating underflow. The complete `admit_regional_candidates` function
  body, threshold, alpha allocation and multiplicity correction are unchanged.
  Numeric outcomes may change because the likelihood calculation is repaired.
* The benchmark reporting reader excludes labels at the HDF5 column-selection
  boundary when `response=False`, retaining row order and duplicates.
* Formula recovery preserves the historical bounded comparison boolean, adds
  its comparison scope and an explicit unresolved status for false/unknown.
  A false result is not a proof of inequivalence. No unrestricted symbolic
  simplification or response-based parameter fitting is added.
* quad_vec convergence is checked for decision-risk quadrature. Its error is
  reported as an estimate, with `rigorous_enclosure=False`. A numerically
  separated action is not reported as a rigorous targeted certificate. The
  rigorous Arb paths and historical acquisition authorization remain unchanged.

## Proposal calibration and isolation

The next generation cycle receives only whitelisted development-failure
categories and counts. It also receives the registered input context,
independent gap brief and frozen bank supports. Admission scores, margins,
regional likelihood ratios, selector masses and thresholds are rejected at
transport-facing context boundaries. The inner orchestration projection
excludes full admission/audit artifacts. Carried candidate lineages remain
available as bank members; their admission outcomes are not calibration data.
This is category feedback, not an empirically validated learned calibrator.

## Prospective mechanism accounting

Every cycle records the mechanism funnel with explicit numerators, denominators
and null rates for an empty population. Previously evaluated/carry-over rows
are excluded from new-proposal statistics.

| Quantity | Population |
|---|---|
| domain-valid / typed-preflight-attempts | New typed proposals actually selected for evaluation; compiled directives and unselected counts are reported separately |
| fit-survived / compiled | New LLM evaluation attempts whose syntax compiled, including preflight deaths |
| discovery-accepted / evaluated | Validated inner proposals tested for incumbent replacement; typed proposals have no separate incumbent gate |
| independently-admitted / admission-evaluated | Candidates with actual independent regional predictive-ratio records; duplicate-support exclusions remain in total attempts |

Also record distinct valid support counts, operator-family histograms,
rejection reasons, proposal/source lineages, attempted versus evaluated budgets
and posterior/gap identities. None of these statistics is an efficacy claim.
The final success target is domain-valid yield together with preserved
structural diversity/novelty, followed by independent admission and matched
predictive assessment. Merely reducing nonfinite deaths is insufficient.

## Required next experiment boundary

Before a fresh matched mechanism experiment, freeze a protocol that separates
shared correctness repairs from the proposed validity/calibration upgrade.
Both arms must use the repaired amplitude/log-density/HDF5/metric semantics.
Comparing this upgrade directly with the unpatched public baseline would
confound numerical bug repair with candidate-quality change.

Register fresh tasks/seeds and disjoint role identities before data access.
Use a paired matrix with identical engine frontier, posterior/prior, LLM model
and call policy, proposal/evaluation/label budgets, compute ceiling, role
splits, admission gate and reporting target. Preserve all abstentions,
preflight/fit/admission failures and negative outcomes. No prompt, task or seed
may be optimized against the earlier PO33 diagnostic. Report the funnel first,
then diversity/novelty and paired predictive effects with uncertainty. The
current delivery contains no new efficacy runner or launch command; the
matched protocol requires its own registration/source-composition gate.
