# P3G.1 nuisance-aware C1--C3 mainline contract

Status: **IMPLEMENTED; REAL GATE NOT YET EXECUTED**

## Why this replaces another decision-layer patch

P3B.10, P3C.1, and P3D.2 are protocol-valid negative development evidence.
CCPP remains harmful relative to random even at ordinary Bayes (`eta=1`), and
P3E.3 rejects the registered nominal predictive law in three of eight seeds.
Those facts rule out another score regularizer as the next root repair.  The
failure is upstream: the finite symbolic bank does not represent residual
process uncertainty well enough for its model-relative information score to
transfer reliably to measured outcomes.

P3G.1 therefore returns to the C1--C3 core.  It does not continue the P3F.4
countably-open exact-sampling certification branch, whose CERT.23 result is a
useful correctness appendix but whose operational proposal caps remain far
outside the registered real compute scale.  An open grammar is not required
to test the paper's central estimand, posterior-coherence, and acquisition
claims on the already frozen finite real bank.

## Generative posterior repair

Every symbolic structure `S` receives a spike/slab discrepancy state and one
of two response-independent RBF kernel states.  On the complete registered
covariate domain, the discrepancy factor is projected orthogonally to the
structure design.  A deterministic singular-value ordering retains at most
16 directions.  This rank is a pre-experiment computational sieve shared by
all datasets, targets, seeds, and policies; responses cannot select its basis,
rank, kernels, or priors.

The resulting ordinary Bayesian target jointly integrates structure,
coefficients, discrepancy activation, discrepancy kernel state, discrepancy
coordinates, and noise.  Rank-one Normal--Inverse-Gamma updates are algebraic
equivalents of batch conjugate fitting, so prequential calibration and
sequential acquisition reuse exactly one frozen posterior target.

This design follows two directly relevant findings from the primary
literature.  Sloman et al. show that target-only Bayesian active learning can
suffer negative interference when nuisance variables are poorly identified,
and that nuisance learning is an essential auxiliary objective:
https://proceedings.mlr.press/v244/sloman24a.html.  Orthogonal Gaussian
processes address confounding between a parametric mean and stochastic model
discrepancy: https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.00203.  These sources motivate the
general algorithm; no published dataset result sets a PCPI threshold or
branch.

## One-command sequential Gate

The runner first performs 24 prequential PIT e-process audits: three measured
targets by eight frozen seeds.  The global false-alarm budget is `1/100`, split
equally as `1/2400` per run by a union bound.  Candidate responses and held-out
data remain unavailable during this phase.

If any audit rejects, the terminal decision is `CALIBRATION_NO_GO`; candidate
responses remain sealed and no acquisition row is produced.  If and only if
all 24 audits pass, the same invocation resets every posterior to its initial
32 observations, opens the already committed measured candidate pool, and
runs the 96 matched-budget policy comparisons.

The P3G.1 policy targets the joint frozen variable `(C,D)`, where `C` is the
initial operational predictive-equivalence class and `D` is the discrepancy
spike/kernel nuisance state.  Thus

`I((C,D);Y_a) = I(C;Y_a) + I(D;Y_a | C)`.

It contains no fitted trade-off weight.  Numerical quadrature must certify the
leader; an unresolved ranking uses the independently frozen random reference
draw rather than switching to a different posterior utility.  Random,
predictive uncertainty, and QBC use the same repaired posterior, H0, candidate
pool, label budget, and validation endpoint.

Gas CO and NOX are averaged within seed before family-level paired intervals,
so the 16 target rows are not incorrectly treated as 16 independent seeds.
The held-out role stays closed.  Even a positive development Gate therefore
sets `formal_efficacy_evidence=false`; it can authorize a separately frozen
final confirmation but cannot itself become the paper's final superiority
claim.

## Prohibitions retained

- no simulated response or simulated efficacy result;
- no dataset-name branch in posterior fitting or action selection;
- no outcome-selected rank, kernel, prior, threshold, seed, or retry;
- no validation response in the acquisition posterior or action score;
- no candidate response before the global calibration decision;
- no held-out access; and
- no rewriting of P3B--P3E negative evidence.
