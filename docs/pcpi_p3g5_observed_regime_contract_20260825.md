# PCPI P3G.5 observed-regime contract

P3G.4 is immutable negative development evidence: 24 audits completed, eight
rejected, and acquisition remained closed. Six rejections were Gas CO. The
registered data protocol reveals a structural omission: Gas roles are year
groups, with 2011--2012 development, 2013 validation, 2014 acquisition and
2015 held-out, but the posterior used only nine sensor covariates and discarded
the observed year nuisance.

P3G.5 adds one common linear year column to every Gas structure design. It is
standardized using only the 32 selected development groups and is absent for
CCPP, whose registry provides no group. The year coefficient is integrated in
every structure but does not alter the scientific structure-class partition.
No response constructs the column, and the 2015 held-out group is not opened.

P3G.5 also raises the response-independent `R^2 ~ Uniform(0,1)` Gauss--Legendre
rule from three to five nodes. This is numerical refinement of the registered
continuous prior approximation, not selection of a result-favorable node.

All datasets, seeds, row identities, budgets, calibration bets, alpha
allocation, failure policy and candidate/held-out boundaries remain unchanged.
