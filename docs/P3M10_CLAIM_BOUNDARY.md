# P3M.10 claim boundary

The confirmatory primary estimand is frozen-operational-class decision-risk
superiority only on dataset families that exhibit positive aggregation under the
registered resolution rule. A family with zero aggregation is not deleted or
reclassified; it is reported as non-identifiable negative evidence and is
excluded from the class-superiority denominator. Predictive normalized AULC is
a secondary endpoint reported across all registered families.

This boundary is fixed before confirmation. Threshold mutation, family deletion,
post-hoc reclassification, seed selection, and held-out access are forbidden.
